# Task 9 financial policies and remaining approvals

| Policy | Applied owner approval | Remaining external approval or dependency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — Remove after declining a replacement | A customer can request that only the unavailable line be removed. Eligible unpaid orders continue at the recalculated total. Paid cases retain the original amount and payment references while Assist reviews an indicative difference. | Approve eligibility and amount limits for a partial refund on an active paid order, the provider reconciliation and idempotency procedure, and the payout allocation of the difference. The existing Grocery provider refund route explicitly supports full refunds only on cancelled orders. The Restaurant route also requires the refund to match the original capture. An incremental charge needs an authorized linked payment-intent and capture process for the difference. |
| B — Preserve spent rewards | Admin can approve preserving the existing ledger or reject an adjustment. Customer notification and audit are recorded once. | Approve specific adjustment arithmetic and compensating credit/debit rules before posting any ledger movement, including partial cancellation and coupon/loyalty treatment. |
| C — Professional then Admin review | A verified professional records guidance and the proposed document type. A separate Admin can approve or reject after that review. Approval records are append-only through the application. | An authorized operator must verify professional identity and provision the approved account ID through `COMEIN_VERIFIED_TAX_REVIEWER_IDS`. Case-specific CA guidance and an approved document issuance procedure, numbering, invoice linkage and reconciliation must exist before generating a legal correction. Original invoices remain preserved. |
