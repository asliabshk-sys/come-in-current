# Delivery Partner Allocation — decision record

Every row is **OWNER DECISION REQUIRED**. Options are unselected. Record the signed fee allocation agreement and authoritative order assignment source before enabling partner earnings or settlement.

| Decision | Current evidence | Unselected options and consequences |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Priority among eligible partners | Eligibility exists at the onboarding access boundary. [Audit](AUDIT.md) | Owner-defined rotation, distance/availability priority, or Admin ranking. Specify inputs, ties and fairness. Changes delivery workload and response time. |
| 2. Assignment mode | No assignment API or provider. [Audit](AUDIT.md) | Manual, automatic, or assisted with audited override. Define assignment authority and concurrency obligations. |
| 3. Partner rejection | No assignment acceptance or rejection state. [Audit](AUDIT.md) | Allow rejection before acceptance or define a later cutoff and escalation. Specify timeout and notice. |
| 4. Reassignment earnings | Partner payable is explicitly unavailable. [Tests](TESTS.md) | Keep original earnings pending review, or document allocation by verified milestone. Define which party funds any split. |
| 5. Cancellation before pickup | Customer cancellation exists; partner linkage does not. [Audit](AUDIT.md) | Approve no partner adjustment or a documented exception. Specify proof and whether costs are owed. |
| 6. After pickup or failed delivery | No partner-stage failure record. [Audit](AUDIT.md) | Case review only, or written rules per verified cause and milestone. Impacts partner and customer costs. |
| 7. Deductions, bonuses or adjustments | No approved partner adjustment implementation. [Audit](AUDIT.md) | Exclude them or approve exact trigger, caps, evidence and reversals. Affects payable and dispute exposure. |
| 8. Delivery-fee refund allocation | Source fees are preserved and Admin can compare current values. [Tests](TESTS.md) | Review each case, or approve an explicit partner/platform allocation and funding source by eligible refund outcome. |
| 9. Manual adjustment authority | Admin may review evidence; there is no partner payable mutation. [Audit](AUDIT.md) | Specify requester, approver, finance verification and separation of duties. Record old/new values, proof and idempotency. |
| 10. Disputes | No assignment dispute workflow. [Audit](AUDIT.md) | Define intake, evidence freeze, timeline, appeal and release authority. Balances operational effort and protected funds. |
