# Tax and reconciliation checklist

- Compare accepted order, original invoice, source tax details, charged amounts, replacement decisions and ledger event timestamps.
- Match online order and capture identifiers, currency, original capture, prior refund references and confirmed, pending or failed statuses separately.
- Compare COD fulfillment, cash confirmation and matching audit. Require actual collection and payout proof independently of merchant attestation and order amount.
- Reconcile merchant and platform records with original document and any authorized correction. Preserve history and document coupon and reward treatment separately.
- Confirm case-specific taxable value and correction direction in writing. Use original approved evidence and CA instructions. GST Engine remains out of scope.
