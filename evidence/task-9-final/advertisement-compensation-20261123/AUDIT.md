# Advertisement compensation audit — 23 November 2026

**Overall: PARTIALLY VERIFIED.** Reviewed source, accepted-term and audit data models, the seller-facing flows and APIs, and synthetic MongoDB records. Starting work ref was `8b0707afa47f96fece06f4d1a8f565a866df1505`. This run used disposable synthetic records and no production data.

| Area | Status | Verification and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Booking and accepted terms | VERIFIED | Merchant creates a campaign draft with schedule and placement; seller screens display configured terms. Shop ownership controls submission and acceptance. Acceptance stores actor, timestamp and version on that campaign. Fixed fee, dates, placement, targeting and optional impression target are captured. Rate/budget conflicts and stale terms fail; accepted terms cannot be superseded. Terms contain no approved compensation schedule. |
| B. Compensation eligibility | OWNER DECISION REQUIRED | The delivery report distinguishes in-progress, target met, under-delivery and over-delivery. Zero and partial delivery are distinguishable by recorded count against target. Rejection, Admin pause and removal are campaign states. Advertiser cancellation, platform cancellation as a distinct commercial workflow, and failure attribution are not implemented. There is no compensation entitlement or execution route. |
| C. Delivery evidence | PARTIALLY VERIFIED | Authenticated impression/click submissions use canonical campaign IDs, active window and approval checks. Commercial terms enforce configured context. Mongo deduplicates by campaign, user, event type and UTC day. These are recorded events, not independently witnessed displays or campaign-wide unique customers. Geographical and off-platform corroboration require separate evidence. |
| D. Shortfall and excess | VERIFIED | Configured target is compared with in-window counts. Ended-campaign shortfall and over-delivery are independent fields. No monetary value or automatic extension is computed. |
| E. Invalid/disputed evidence | PARTIALLY VERIFIED | Booking reference claims are unique. Final review checks campaign ownership of evidence, accepted terms version, fee and caps. Cross-campaign/mismatched evidence requires reconciliation. Admin-only reviewed snapshot is immutable and identical request replays return it. A formal advertiser dispute, adjustment approval and resolution workflow are absent. |
| F. Cancellation and refunds | PARTIALLY VERIFIED | Admin rejection, pause and removal save actor, reason, timestamp and transition together. Repeated identical immediate requests do not duplicate audit rows. Removed/rejected campaigns cannot be moved through pause to resume. Financial booking history is retained. Advertiser self-cancellation and commercial refund/credit are not implemented. |
| G. Limits and integrity | PARTIALLY VERIFIED | Fixed budget reservation, spend record and release use Mongo transactions. Accepted fixed fee must fit daily/total caps. A claimed reference cannot be reused. Reconciliation alone cannot post adjustments. There is no approved compensation cap because compensation is disabled. Admin-recorded booking denotes evidence linkage and is not independent settlement proof. |
| H. Moderation and Admin approval | VERIFIED | Poster validation, CHAND review of exact poster bytes, and designated owner/Admin publication gate remain. Unapproved, removed, paused, rejected or out-of-window campaigns cannot record delivery events. The separate delivery review route cannot publish content. |

External booking/payment confirmation, settlement and object-storage validation are **DEFERRED — POST ALL APP TASKS**. Tests use mocked storage and local transactions. The [decision record](OWNER-DECISION.md) lists the outstanding commercial rules.
