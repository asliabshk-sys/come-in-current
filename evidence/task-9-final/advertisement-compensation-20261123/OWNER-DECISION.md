# Advertisement compensation — owner decision record

Every item remains **OWNER DECISION REQUIRED**. These options are unselected. Each decision needs approved wording, responsible party, scope, effective date and written evidence. A commercial change affecting accepted terms also needs the appropriate advertiser agreement and finance confirmation.

| Decision | Existing evidence | Options for explicit approval | Financial and operational impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Eligibility after shortfall | Final snapshot records target and shortfall. | Keep review-only, or authorize a narrowly defined remedy for an ended campaign with verified delivery and funding evidence. Define excluded causes. | Eligibility could create a new platform obligation and review workload. |
| 2. Partial and zero delivery | Count and target distinguish zero, partial and full delivery. | Handle zero/partial via separate rules, or use one rule with explicitly approved thresholds and rounding. Confirm treatment of target-met and excess delivery. | Thresholds affect customer remedy and expected financial exposure. |
| 3. Remedy type | No posting or extension is available. | Explicitly choose review only, extension, credit, adjustment or refund and specify any allowed combinations and consent. | Extension affects placement capacity; financial remedies require ledger, confirmation and funding rules. |
| 4. Advertiser cancellation | No advertiser self-cancellation or commercial cancellation rules exist. | Keep manual review, or define pre-start/in-flight cutoff, evidence, incurred cost and treatment of prior bookings. | Changes cancellation rights, platform exposure and support process. |
| 5. Platform cancellation and technical failure | Admin pause/reject/remove is audited, but commercial cause attribution is absent. | Keep case-by-case review, or approve cause categories and eligibility by stage, including technical outage evidence and moderation rejection. | Separates platform-fault obligations from moderation actions and advertiser actions. |
| 6. Evidence | Campaign-bound accepted terms, immutable delivery snapshot and unique booking claim are locally queryable. | Specify admissible evidence, independent provider/payment or invoice proof, measurement window, event validation and fault attribution. | Stronger verification adds review steps. Weak evidence raises false or duplicated payout risk. |
| 7. Authorization | Designated Admin approves publication; Admin records delivery review. | Specify who requests, reviews, authorizes and executes financial relief, and whether separate finance sign-off is mandatory. | Separates content moderation from payment authority and sets accountability. |
| 8. Limit and funding | Fixed campaign caps limit booking only. | Explicitly approve whether a remedy cap applies, its basis, funding source, cumulative cap, taxes, rounding and reversal. | Determines maximum liability and reconciliation requirements. |
| 9. Disputes and complaints | Mismatched evidence is flagged. There is no dedicated compensation appeals flow. | Specify intake, response window, evidence freeze, decision record, appeals and advertiser notification. | Requires support ownership and safeguards against repeat requests and disputed evidence posting. |
