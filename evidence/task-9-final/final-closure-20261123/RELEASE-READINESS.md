# Task 9 release readiness

| Category | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Internally verified | Applicable approved local controls passed fresh regression and persistence checks. See [integration evidence](INTEGRATION-TESTS.md). |
| Owner approval pending | Commercial expansion, recovery and eligibility decisions listed in the [owner handoff](REMAINING-OWNER-DECISIONS.md). |
| Professional approval pending | Original invoice treatment, corrected values, legal issuer and formal correction authorization. See the unsigned [CA packet](../ca-preparation-20261123/CA-REVIEW-SUMMARY.md). |
| External validation deferred | Device, payments, identity, fraud, storage, partner/provider and actual CA/issuance evidence in the [deferred list](DEFERRED-EXTERNAL-VALIDATION.md). |
| Internal implementation incomplete | Independent replacement expiry/cancellation; authorized expanded coupon checkout and paid refund effects; ad remedy; partner allocation and settlement; reward compensation; real incremental and partial/COD execution; statutory issuance. |
| Release-blocking issues | Do not represent any pending policy or external operation as ready. Any rollout depending on these operations is blocked pending decisions, resulting implementation and actual validation. Local tests alone do not certify production readiness. |
