# Remaining financial policy approvals

| Area | Safe implementation today | Required before execution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incremental payment | Customer can see the repriced total and consent to review of the difference. Admin can approve or reject the immutable server-quoted amount. | Approve provider support for a linked incremental intent and capture, reconcile it with the original charge, and verify idempotency, credentials and TEST execution. |
| Full, partial, and COD refund | Existing full refund workflow retained. Replacement removal can produce a pending execution approval for a server quote. A case that cannot safely be priced stays in Assist. | Approve active-order partial-refund eligibility, COD refund rails and proof of receipt, provider idempotency and reconciliation, and treatment of other open or prior refunds. |
| Seller and delivery accounting | Existing confirmed-order ledger and seller settlement controls remain in place. | Approve replacement allocation, eligible refunds and reversals, delivery partner agreement and approved payout split, beneficiary reconciliation and payout verification. |
| Platform revenue | Existing order earnings and separate verified membership reporting remain in place. | Identify recognized sponsored-ad and other income source records, approved revenue recognition and reconciliation rules. No new revenue figures are assumed. |
| Reward, coupon and referral | Existing shopping/referral ledger, referral attribution and customer protection remain in place. New Admin draft validation records budget and policy without creating spendable value. | Approve each campaign's funding, rate, segment and restrictions, publish terms, and authorize checkout binding, transactional redemption and exhaustion handling. Approve referral reversal and compensating ledger policy. |
| Tax and invoices | Assigned, active professional access and separate Admin decisions are audited. Original invoices stay intact. | Provision independently verified professional accounts with role `tax_professional`, `tax_review_active=true` and membership in `COMEIN_VERIFIED_TAX_REVIEWER_IDS`. Approve case-specific accounting treatment, issuer identity, legal numbering, issuance, reconciliation and filing before producing a document. |
